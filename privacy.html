Privacy policy.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 11, 2026

Updraft is an offline arcade game developed by Ian Dorosh. This policy describes the game, this support website, and email support.

The game

Updraft does not send gameplay information or personal data to us. It does not contain accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, or in-app purchases. No internet connection is required to play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best height, unlocked and selected pilots, and your sound, haptic, and tilt-control preferences on your device. We do not receive these local saves.

Deleting the app removes its local app data. Copies in device backups are managed by your device and backup settings and may be restored with a backup.

Motion controls

When tilt steering is enabled during gameplay, Updraft uses your device’s motion sensors to steer the character. These readings are processed on your device in real time. The app does not save a history of motion readings or transmit them to us. You can turn tilt off in Settings and use drag controls instead.

Email support

If you email Ian.dorosh@outlook.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

Support messages are kept only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or as required by law. You may email us to request deletion of your support correspondence.

The website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, account registration, or contact forms.

GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to provide and secure hosting. GitHub’s own privacy practices apply to that processing. Read the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Sharing and services

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising. The game does not share gameplay or motion information with third parties.

Email is handled by the email service providers involved in your message; GitHub provides website hosting. Apple handles App Store distribution and any system services under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

Children

The game does not ask players for personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support email, contact us so we can address the request.

Your choices and questions

You can change gameplay preferences in Settings, manage local app data and backups through your device, and contact us about support information you have sent.

Questions or privacy requests: Ian.dorosh@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app, website, or support practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
