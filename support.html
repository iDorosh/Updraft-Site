How to play

  1. Your pilot bounces automatically. Tilt to steer left and right, or drag anywhere in the playfield.
  2. Land on platforms to keep climbing. Cross either side of the screen to wrap around to the other.
  3. Avoid enemy bodies and glowing orbs. Land on an enemy from above while falling to knock it out and bounce.
  4. Use your power buttons when you need them. Rare power cells restore one charge; a full power inventory hides its matching cells.
  5. Beat your personal best. The higher you climb, the more challenging the route becomes.

Can I turn tilt steering off?

Yes. Open Settings from the hangar and uncheck “Tilt to steer.” Drag controls remain available either way. Tilt is enabled by default for new players, and your saved choice is kept between sessions.

How do I unlock the other pilots?

Volt is ready from the start. Reach a personal best of 1,500 metres to unlock Glitch, then 4,500 metres to unlock Ember. Earned pilots stay unlocked.

  • Volt: Boost launches you upward.
  • Glitch: Boost plus Phase, which protects against enemies and orbs for ten seconds.
  • Ember: Boost, Phase, and Pulse, which clears nearby enemies and hostile orbs.

Phase protects against hazards, but it cannot save a missed landing.

What do the platform colours mean?

  • Lime: steady footing.
  • Violet: a moving platform.
  • Coral: one bounce, then it breaks.
  • Cyan: a spring platform with extra lift.
  • Amber: a conveyor that pushes your bounce sideways.
  • Teal: a magnetic pad that helps guide a nearby falling pilot.
  • Pink: a blink platform that flashes before disappearing. Land while it is solid.

Why did the game pause?

Updraft pauses when you leave the app or when the playfield resizes, including when folding, unfolding, or rotating a supported device. Tap “Keep going” to resume the run.

Where is my progress saved?

Your best height, unlocked pilots, and preferences are saved locally on your device. No account is required. Deleting the app removes its local data; your device’s backup settings determine whether a backup copy is available.

Which devices can play?

Updraft is designed for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 and later. You can play without an internet connection.

A note on privacy

The game does not use ads, analytics, accounts, or tracking. Read the privacy policy for details about local saves, motion controls, support email, and website hosting.
